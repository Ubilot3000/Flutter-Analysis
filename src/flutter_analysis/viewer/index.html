MODEL INPUTS & INTERPRETATION

Each semispan has bending and twist coordinates and Jones wake lags. The center is fixed; boom motion and structural coupling across the root are omitted. The random vertical gust is a first-order colored process with an illustrative 0.5 m length scale and 0.75 left/right correlation in mixed mode. Optional periodic airflow illustrates a wake; its amplitude and frequency need measurements. This is not a calibrated Dryden atmosphere or a separate Sears gust model. Loop repeats the generated record and resets at its beginning.

The NACA display thickness does not supply material stiffness. Responses stop at the small-motion limit (10% semispan displacement or 10° twist); nonlinear saturation alone does not establish a physical limit cycle. The vertical stabilizer is outside this model.
